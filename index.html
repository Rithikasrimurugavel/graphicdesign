Strawberry Soda — Flat Label

Strawberry Soda — Packaging Label Design A complete beverage label design developed for a fictional strawberry soda brand, Fruitsoda. The concept uses a vibrant strawberry-focused visual identity with a pink and red color palette to communicate freshness, sweetness, and a youthful personality. The label was designed as a 360° packaging system, covering the front, back, and side panels. The front panel focuses on strong visual branding and product appeal, while the back and side panels organize essential product information such as nutrition facts, composition, benefits, expiry details, barcode, and customer-care information. Design Focus: Brand Identity • Packaging Layout • Typography • Visual Hierarchy • Information Design
