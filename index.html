About

Designer by craft,
storyteller by instinct.

I’m a graphic designer and final-year Computer Science student who moves comfortably between clean commercial layouts and expressive, illustrated storytelling. Whether it’s a café ad, a brand mark, or a fully illustrated poster, I start from the same question: what should someone feel in the first second?

My hand-drawn illustration work — including original character art and webtoon-style pieces — keeps my instinct for pacing, colour and emotion sharp, and that instinct feeds back into every commercial brief I take on.
